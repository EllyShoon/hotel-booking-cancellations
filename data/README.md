## Note on the subset used

This analysis used a **34,210-row extract covering arrivals in 2016 only**. The
full published dataset contains roughly 119,000 bookings across 2015-2017.

The cancellation rates reported here — 86% for city hotels against 22% for resort
hotels — are specific to that extract and are **substantially wider than the gap
in the full dataset**, where both sit far closer together. Do not quote these
figures as properties of the published dataset. See the limitations section of
the README.
